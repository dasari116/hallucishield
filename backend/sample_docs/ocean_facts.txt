The ocean covers about 71 percent of Earth's surface and contains most of the planet's water. The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean basin. Ocean currents move heat, nutrients, and organisms around the planet, and they are driven by wind, differences in water density, and the rotation of Earth. Coral reefs support diverse marine communities but are sensitive to changes in temperature, light, and water chemistry. The deepest known parts of the ocean are found in hadal trenches, including the Mariana Trench in the western Pacific.
