The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite and is the fifth-largest satellite in the Solar System. It orbits Earth at an average distance of about 384,400 kilometers. Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the "Red Planet" due to iron oxide on its surface. Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. The International Space Station orbits Earth at an altitude of roughly 400 kilometers and completes an orbit approximately every 90 minutes. Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and has a Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has been observed for over 350 years.
