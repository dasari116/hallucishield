Earth's climate is influenced by the energy it receives from the Sun and by how that energy moves through the atmosphere, oceans, land, and ice. Carbon dioxide, methane, and water vapor are greenhouse gases that absorb and re-emit infrared energy, warming the lower atmosphere. Burning coal, oil, and natural gas adds carbon dioxide to the atmosphere. Oceans absorb much of the excess heat in the climate system and also take up carbon dioxide, which contributes to ocean acidification. Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions, while climate describes patterns measured over much longer periods.
